# Supplier Findings: Multi-Vendor Ordering Platform
Verified against live sites and company records. All below are currently trading and dealer/trade-account based, not retail.

**Model confirmed:** every supplier below sells on a Collect basis to trade accounts, courier (BobGo) handles delivery, no stock held by us.

---

## 1. Front-of-house & kitchen uniforms
**Amrod** (Johannesburg, Cape Town, Durban, Gqeberha) — Africa's largest trade-only apparel/promo supplier. Owns Altitude and other house brands: aprons, chef wear, golf shirts, caps. In-house embroidery/branding.
- Process: online trade registration, vetted before approval.
- ⚠️ Their own site states they may reject applicants not "primarily promotional product resellers." Apply, but don't assume yes.
- Backup if rejected: **Grand Uniforms** (Durban) or **The Promo Group**, both resell the same brand ranges (Bova, Lemaitre, Altitude, Chef Works) without Amrod's stricter vetting.

**Jonsson Workwear** — national SA brand, heavy-duty chef jackets, kitchen staff uniforms, industrial-grade. Corporate/bulk account structure available through their Corporate Sales division (Durban HQ, Gauteng hubs). Confirmed real and actively trading nationwide.

## 2. Health & Safety / PPE (wet floor signs, gloves, safety footwear)
**Dromex** — confirmed largest PPE and workwear wholesaler in Southern Africa, 25,000m² Durban (Cornubia) distribution facility, founded 1998. Oven mitts, chemical gloves, goggles, wet floor signage.
- Process: apply direct for trade account first.
- ⚠️ Dromex sells mostly through regional distributors (Fowkes Bros, Trust Health & Safety, Toolcraft, BMG). If direct account is declined, go to one of these, same stock, same brand.

**Beier Group / BBF Safety Group** — confirmed largest PPE manufacturer in Africa, owns Bova, Lemaitre, Bronx brands. For non-slip kitchen safety boots.
- Process: apply for wholesale account, or go through an established distributor like **Treadsafe** (KZN-founded, national reach) who already carries the full Bova/Lemaitre range.

## 3. Kitchen smallwares, utensils, cutlery, and heavy equipment
**BCE Foodservice Equipment** (Roodepoort, Johannesburg) — Southern Africa's leading kitchen utensil/cookware/appliance supplier. Established 1986/87, 35+ years, 6000+ products, 60+ brands, dealer-only network. Their catalogue explicitly covers utensils, buffetware, AND tableware, meaning **cutlery lives here too**, alongside pizza cutters, rolling pins, stainless inserts, scales, timers, turners.
- Process: submit Dealer Application via bce.co.za. Courier collects direct from the Roodepoort distribution plant.
- This is your one-stop for the largest single chunk of the Debonairs smalls list, cutlery included, without needing a separate cutlery specialist.

Backup/benchmark only, not primary: **The Restaurant Warehouse** (for price comparison during vendor evaluation), **Wechsler** (distributes Eetrite, SA's longest-standing cutlery brand, useful if BCE's cutlery pricing or range comes up short).

## 4. Heavy-duty plastics, dough trays, bins, crates
**Mpact Plastic Containers** — confirmed JSE-listed (via Mpact Group), the largest industrial plastic moulder in Southern Africa. Plants in Atlantis, Brits, Castleview. Already manufactures bread crates as standard stock; custom colour and food-grade runs available on bulk orders.
- Process: apply for a direct cash trade account with their distribution division.
- This is the highest-value call to make. Dough trays alone were 36% of the sample Debonairs order (R35,550 of R99,662). Get a like-for-like quote on the 600x400 dough tray spec before anything else.
- Courier note: plastic bins/trays are bulky. BobGo collection needs volumetric sizing confirmed per SKU before this account goes live in the system.

## 5. Hygiene & janitorial (mops, buckets, brooms, wringers, bins)
**Bidvest Steiner** — JSE-listed (via Bidvest Group), 39 branches nationwide, trading since 1958. HACCP colour-coded ranges exist specifically for food environments. Free door-to-door delivery is their standard model, worth checking how that interacts with a Collect-only account structure.
- Process: contact for a business/janitorial consumables account.
